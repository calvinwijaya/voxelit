Roads & Cars
—
Roads come from OpenStreetMap. The cars are a simple simulation, not live traffic: they wander the road network at typical speeds for each road type, follow one-way streets, keep to the local side of the road and turn their headlights on at night.
Fire Spread
Spread uses the current wind (live or manual). Humidity follows live data until you move the slider. Rain slows the fire. Embers jump downwind above 5 m/s.
Basemap
The map surface follows the terrain under the voxels and continues it smoothly outside.
