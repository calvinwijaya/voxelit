How it works

01

Pick a spot

Search for a place or zoom into the world map and click. Choose a 0.5, 1 or 2 km square.

02

Open data comes in

Buildings, roads and water from OpenStreetMap, terrain from elevation tiles, trees and grass read from satellite imagery.

03

Your browser voxelizes

Everything becomes 5 m blocks on your own device — no server. Save the result as a .vxl.gz file.
